## Voiceover, clean read (for the recording)

> Student life is expensive. Lunch, transport, data… it adds up.
>
> Meet Unipicks: deals from local spots near campus, made just for students. Sign up with your Kepler email.
>
> Browse deals. Order in two taps. Group up with friends for better prices. Pay with MoMo. Done.
>
> Share a deal with your friends, and watch your savings add up.
>
> Join Unipicks. Save more. Eat better.

**Delivery notes**
- Use a warm, smiling, conversational voice: a friend sharing a tip, not an advert. A student voice from Kepler is ideal and builds authenticity.
- Stress **expensive**, **students**, **two taps**, **friends**, **save**.
- In 0:12–0:20 the four beats are 2 s each. Leave a short breath between them so each line lands on its scene change (14.0 s, 16.0 s, 18.0 s).
- The final three-part line should land on the three text reveals at 27.2 s, 27.55 s and 27.9 s.
