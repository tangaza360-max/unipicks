## 🔴 Three things to settle before launch

1. **UmunotaPay's fee.** If it is more than 1.5% and Unipicks pays it,
   Unipicks loses money on every order (`revenue-model.md` §3).
2. **Paying the businesses.** All payments land in Unipicks' UmunotaPay
   wallet; each business must know when and how it gets its 98.5%.
3. **The merchant agreement** (collecting on the business's behalf, the 1.5%,
   refunds) — with the lawyer.

## The 90-day plan (Mon 12 Oct 2026 → Sun 10 Jan 2027)

❓ Check Kepler's term dates: if there is a holiday in December or early
January, move the student push (weeks 5–8) so it happens during term.

| Weeks | Dates | Goal | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1–2** | 12 – 25 Oct | **Learn.** Add the "how often" question; send the survey with a reminder after 3 days. Ask UmunotaPay its fee and payout process. Write down monthly costs (`revenue-model.md` §4). | ≥ 100 survey answers; go/no-go ≥ 60%; UmunotaPay answered |
| **3–4** | 26 Oct – 8 Nov | **Sign food places.** Visit the places students named. Use the pitch in `go-to-market.md` §3. Help each one create a priced deal. | **3** approved businesses with live deals |
| **5–6** | 9 – 22 Nov | **Soft launch.** Invite the survey's beta testers (Q7 emails). Recruit 3 ambassadors. Fix what testers report. | 50 monthly active students; order success ≥ 80% |
| **7–8** | 23 Nov – 6 Dec | **Campus launch.** Launch email, posters with QR codes, class WhatsApp groups, a "first lunch" deal at each partner. | **5** active businesses; 100 monthly active students |
| **9–10** | 7 – 20 Dec | **Build the habit.** Weekly deals at slow hours, group orders, ask for reviews and photos. Pay ambassador bonuses for students who order twice. | Repeat rate ≥ 30% |
| **11–13** | 21 Dec – 10 Jan | **Measure and decide.** Run the queries in `metrics.md`. Short follow-up survey for beta users. Decide: next campus, or fix Kepler first. Revisit the fee with real numbers (`revenue-model.md` §5). | **Gate:** repeat rate ≥ 40% and 8+ active businesses → plan ALU + one UR campus |

## What is not in this plan (on purpose)

- **Machine learning** (Area 5): too few students yet; revisit at about 300
  active students (founder decision, 2026-10-10).
- **Other universities before day 90.**
- **Paid ads.**
